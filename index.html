Our story

We know what makes talent magnetic.

AfrodizzyCasts was born from AfrodizzyActs—an entertainment agency with performance at its heart.

That background gives us a different eye for casting. We understand movement, stagecraft, individuality and the hard-to-name quality that makes someone impossible to look away from.

Our focus is performance artists, with actors and extras sourced when the brief calls for them. Every project starts with listening and ends with people who feel right for the story.
