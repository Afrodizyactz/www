Privacy notice

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This notice explains how AfrodizzyCasts handles personal information submitted through this website. It is intended to support transparent processing under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Information we receive

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, contact details, company or production name, talent requirements and any project information you choose to provide. Talent applications may also include profile information and portfolio links sent by email.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to casting enquiries, evaluate potential talent, coordinate casting services, maintain relevant business records and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only where reasonably necessary to respond to an enquiry, evaluate a casting opportunity, deliver an agreed service or comply with law. We retain information only for as long as it remains relevant to these purposes or is required by law.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. You may also object to certain processing or withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@afrodizzycasts.co.za. Please include enough detail for us to understand and verify your request.
